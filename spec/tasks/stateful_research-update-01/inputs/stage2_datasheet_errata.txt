ERRATA: Revision 1.0 listed a single endurance figure for the whole X900 series
in error.
  X900 1 TB: 300 TBW
  X900 2 TB: 600 TBW
Revision 1.0 is withdrawn. Warranty terms follow the per-model TBW above.
