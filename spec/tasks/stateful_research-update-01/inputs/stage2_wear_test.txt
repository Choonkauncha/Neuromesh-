SOURCE: StorageLab.example independent wear-out test of the X900 1 TB
PUBLISHED: 2026-05-02
METHOD: sequential-write wear test to SMART critical-warning threshold, 2 units,
own logs published.

Both 1 TB units reached the SMART wear threshold at 312 TB and 318 TB written.
The vendor's corrected rating for this model (300 TBW, per errata AE-2026-03)
is consistent with these results. The earlier 600 TBW figure was not reached by
either unit.
